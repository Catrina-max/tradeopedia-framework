# SEC Contract Clause Review Protocol (draft v0.1)

1. Register exhibit URL, exhibit number, parties, and effective date.
2. Review operative text rather than the table of contents alone.
3. Record the section reference, finding, source link, and a separate limitation/ambiguity.
4. Use `verified_source` for a provision mapped to actual text, **not** a verified legal conclusion.
5. Use `dependency_unresolved` for incorporation by reference or missing schedules; `not_determined` when no complete review has established presence or absence.
6. Do not assert that a contract party is the statutory importer of record merely because it is a purchaser.
7. Do not infer arbitration from a generic dispute-resolution heading.
8. Do not equate Incoterms cost/risk allocation with CBP statutory liability.
9. Preserve version and amendments; do not use public contract exhibits as evidence of actual transaction performance.
10. Review findings against full document and associated agreements before labeling the contract completely reviewed.
