Research questions

  • Who undertakes importation, customs entry, duty payment, record retention, and cooperation obligations—and are these parties the statutory importer of record?
  • How do change-in-law, tariff changes, origin representations, warranties, indemnities, and audit rights interact?
  • What are the chosen governing law, CISG treatment, forum, arbitration rules, seat, and enforcement pathways?
  • What documents and event data would be needed to test an importer’s contractual recourse against a supplier?

Interpretation boundaries

SEC exhibits may be redacted, amended, supplemented, or context-dependent. A public supply contract is not necessarily a U.S. import contract. Contractual allocations of loss do not displace public-law customs obligations; commercial arbitration and customs protests operate through distinct legal pathways.
