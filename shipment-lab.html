Shipment Trace Lab

Follow the evidence chain from purchase order to CBP entry and potential supplier remedies. This demonstrator uses invented transaction information; it does not access ACE, verify a CBP entry, or determine legal liability.

Two separate pathways. A CBP matter involving the importer of record is a public-law process; any supplier reimbursement claim is a distinct private-law analysis. A commercial arbitration agreement does not resolve a customs protest.

Transaction architecture

flowchart LR PO[Purchase order] --> INV[Commercial invoice] INV --> ENTRY[Entry / IOR evidence] ENTRY --> CBP[CBP process] INV --> ORIGIN[Origin & HTS support] PO --> CONTRACT[Operative contract clauses] CBP --> PUBLIC[Public-law review] CONTRACT --> PRIVATE[Private supplier remedies] ORIGIN --> PUBLIC ORIGIN --> PRIVATE

Research boundaries

Not knowing an IOR is not evidence that the buyer was or was not IOR. A hypothetical invoice does not establish origin, admissibility, classification, valuation or liquidation. A potential contract claim is not a prediction of indemnification, limitation of liability, litigation or arbitration.
