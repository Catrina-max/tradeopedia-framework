Why this agreement matters

Sections 7.2 and 16.1(a) allow the research model to separate shipment delivery risk from customs duties of the importer of record. Article 10 and 11 add private liability allocation. Article 13 and Section 18.7 introduce an incorporation-by-reference dependency that must be resolved before a dispute forum is categorized.
