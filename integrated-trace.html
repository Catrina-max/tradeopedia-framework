Integrated shipment → clause trace

This executable research demonstrator joins a synthetic customs transaction to a first-pass, source-linked clause map from the SEC-filed Owlet / Shenzhen Aoni agreement. It does not represent an actual shipment, claim, or legal determination.

Two distinct pathways: CBP entry, duties and review remain public-law questions; contractual reimbursement and dispute forum require a separate private-law analysis.

Research model

flowchart LR S["Synthetic shipment evidence"] --> E["Evidence gaps / IOR verification"] S --> C["SEC clause-map references"] E --> P["Public-law review: CBP / CIT"] C --> D["Dependency / applicability review"] D --> R["Possible private claim / dispute forum"]

Methodological limits

Source-linked clauses are potentially relevant research leads, not findings of applicability. The contract’s actual operative status, purchase-order terms, amendments, customs entry, supplier conduct, causation, notice and recoverable damages remain unverified. The engine cannot establish a statutory importer-of-record or a binding arbitration obligation.
