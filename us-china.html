U.S.–China Supply Contracts

Five accession-linked SEC exhibits for studying how U.S. purchasers and Chinese manufacturing or sourcing counterparties document supply obligations, evidence custody and private dispute remedies. This is a curated source list, not a claim of current commercial relationships.

Evidence boundary. Only CN-001 has a first-pass clause map. The others are source verified, not provision-reviewed. No importer-of-record status, customs violation or enforceable recovery outcome is asserted.

Research priorities

  1. Confirm the importer of record from transaction-level evidence, not corporate identity alone.
  2. Trace Incoterms, purchase-order priority, supplier warranties, record retention, and customs-document responsibilities.
  3. Resolve incorporated schedules and internal cross-reference inconsistencies.
  4. Classify international dispute pathways without conflating CBP appeals with supplier arbitration.
