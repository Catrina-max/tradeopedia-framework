3M / Solventum: Reciprocal Supply Contracts and the Separation Agreement

SEC-004: 3M supplies Solventum. SEC-008: Solventum supplies 3M. Both supply agreements incorporate provisions from the same Separation and Distribution Agreement (SDA).

Finding: The SDA resolves the previously unidentified dispute-resolution framework. Article VII provides layered negotiations and CPR mediation; the subsequent route depends on the disputed subject matter. Specified disputes proceed to binding arbitration, while some other disputes may proceed to Delaware litigation. Do not classify all disputes as arbitration. Some schedules were omitted from public filing, limiting issue-specific routing conclusions.

SDA dispute-resolution sequence

§ 7.1

Initial written notice to Transition Committee; 30-day resolution window.

§ 7.2

Officer negotiation; 30-day window after request.

§ 7.3

CEO negotiation; 30-day window after request.

§ 7.4

CPR mediation; Minnesota unless otherwise agreed; mediation and litigation exceptions.

§ 7.5

Binding arbitration for enumerated matters under Schedule 7.5; other eligible disputes may be litigated.

§ 10.2

Delaware governing law; Delaware courts for litigation subject to the dispute rules.

What remains unverified

Schedule 7.4 and Schedule 7.5 are referenced by the SDA, but complete operative terms, omitted schedules, affiliate sub-agreements and transaction facts must be checked before reaching a final forum or liability conclusion. No position is taken on CISG applicability or on which entity actually entered any shipment as importer of record.
